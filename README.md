Everyone sits in their own booth on the set of a live game show. There are no menus: the CRT on
your desk, the four monitors behind the mic, the ON AIR sign, the red button and the telephone
are the whole interface.

## How a round plays

1. **A task card turns over.**
2. **Players bid** on how many they can name out loud.
3. **Someone calls it** on the standing claim.
4. **The claimant performs live** — on the mic, in front of everyone.
5. **A drawn jury counts and votes.** Fame moves accordingly.

Penalty cards and a sabotage market sit on top, so the scoreboard never stays safe for long.

## What's working

- Rules engine, penalty cards
- Online lobby, peer-to-peer netcode and in-game voice chat
- Eight booths, counters and buttons, CRT and broadcast view, light show, on-air clock
- Eleven characters seated in their booths and walking to the mic
- Live Turkish ⇄ English language switching

Menus, settings, music and the finale ceremony are next.
